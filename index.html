ABOUT GDCHS

A place to learn, serve and heal.

Government Dental College, Silchar is a government dental institution in Silchar, Assam. The college was established in 2018 and provides undergraduate dental education.

This website is a modern informational front-end. Official notices, admissions and regulatory information should always be verified from the concerned government authorities.
